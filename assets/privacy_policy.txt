PRIVACY POLICY
(Enterprise Employee Self-Service App – C)
Last Updated: 21-May-2026

1. Introduction
NSP Solutions Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us", "our") provides the ResourcePlus ESS mobile application ("App") as an enterprise employee self-service tool. This App is not for public use. It is made available only to employees of organizations ("Client Companies") that have licensed and deployed our HR & Payroll system.
By using this App, you confirm that you are an employee of a Client Company, and you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Who This App Is For
This App is strictly for current employees of Client Companies using our HR & Payroll platform.
The App cannot be used by the general public.
Login credentials are provided to you by your employer. You do not register for the app independently. Your employer manages your username, password, and access rights.
If your employment ends, your employer will revoke your credentials and your access will be terminated. You must uninstall the App.

3. Information We Collect
a) Information You Submit via Webview (HR Portal)
You may submit the following HR-related requests and forms:
Leave requests
Letter requests
Card requests
General requests
Exceptional attendance requests
b) Information You View (Displayed from Your Employer’s HR System)
Through the webview (HR Portal), you can view the following information pulled from your employer’s HR & Payroll system:
Employee self-service details such as:
Service days
Leave balance
Document expiry notifications
Employee official details (e.g., designation, department, contact information)
Attendance history and records
Attendance Exceptional entry
Employee dashboards and charts
Employees attendance details, reports
Workflow approval tasks, action required tasks details
Manage employee details
Note: This information is stored and controlled by your employer based on the login user’s permission. The app merely displays it and does not store it locally.
c) Information Collected Automatically via Native Features
When you use the native attendance punch feature, the app automatically collects:
Attendance punch data – Date, time, and in/out status
Selfie image – Captured at the time of attendance punch for identity verification
Location coordinates – GPS/network-based location recorded with each attendance punch
Device ID – Unique device identifier for session security and audit trails
Additionally, the app retrieves and displays the following information from your employer’s HR system (this data is not stored locally by the app):
Service days
Leave balance
Document expiry notifications
Employee official details (e.g., designation, department, contact information)
User attendance details (history and records)
d) Session & Preference Data
Cookies – Used for session management within the webview (HR Portal). Cookies are required for the app to function properly.
Local preferences – Language selection (English/Arabic), dark mode, notification settings, and analytics opt-in/out status.
e) Optional Analytics (User Controlled)
You may toggle analytics and data collection on or off within the app’s privacy settings. When enabled, anonymized usage data helps us improve the app.

4. How We Use Your Information
All collected data is used only for legitimate HR, attendance, and payroll processing purposes authorized by your employer, including:
Recording attendance with identity (selfie) and location verification
Displaying leave balances, service days, document expiry, and employee details
Processing leave, letter, card, general, and exceptional attendance requests
Calculating timesheets and payroll inputs
Sending work-related notifications and approvals
Managing workflow tasks

5. Data Sharing
We do not sell or rent your personal data. Data is shared only with:
Your employer (the Client Company) – All data is transmitted to and stored on your employer’s dedicated HR server. Your employer controls access and retention.
Service providers – Limited to essential SDKs (Google Play Services for location & authentication). These providers have their own privacy policies.
Legal authorities – If required by applicable law (UAE or India).

6. Data Storage & Security
Storage location: Your employer’s hosting server (typically in the UAE or as configured by your employer).
Retention: Your data remains until your employer decides to remove it from their HR records (e.g., after employment termination or as required by local labor laws).
Security: Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Device-level security (e.g., biometric login) depends on your device settings.

7. Your Rights and Choices
Because this App is provided through your employer:
Data access, correction, or deletion requests must be directed to your company’s HR department – they are the data controller.
Login credential issues (lost password, reset, or revocation) must be directed to your employer’s HR or IT department.
You also have these technical choices:
Deny camera permission → Selfie capture will not work
Deny location permission → Attendance punch may be blocked
Disable analytics → Toggle off in App Settings → Privacy

8. Legal & Compliance (UAE Focus)
This App is intended for employees of companies operating in the United Arab Emirates (UAE). Your employer is responsible for compliance with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection. We act as a data processor on behalf of your employer.

9. Children’s Privacy
This App is for workplace use only and is not intended for individuals under 13 years of age.

10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted within the App. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
